County Chamber Collective

Chamber Music Retreat

January 14–17, 2027

Prince Edward County, Ontario

Final Concert: Jan 17, 2 PM

The County Chamber Collective brings together a community of passionate musicians for four days of chamber music, collaboration, creativity, and connection in beautiful Prince Edward County.

Taking place January 14–17, 2027, this retreat is an opportunity for musicians to step away from the demands of everyday life, immerse themselves in music, explore new repertoire, and connect with fellow artists in an inspiring and supportive environment.

Rooted in a shared love of chamber music, the retreat encourages curiosity, generosity, and artistic exploration. Through intensive playing, musical collaboration, and professional development, participants are invited to take creative risks, exchange ideas, learn from one another, and expand their musical horizons.

At its heart, the County Chamber Collective believes that exceptional chamber music happens when talented musicians come together, collaborate freely, and genuinely enjoy making music. We are building a welcoming community where the music comes first, friendships flourish, and there's room for plenty of laughter along the way.

No drama. Just great music, good people, and plenty of fun.

The retreat will culminate in a public concert showcasing the musical collaborations and discoveries of the weekend.

This Year's Repertoire

Three Works, One Musical Conversation

At the heart of this year's retreat is a collection of three remarkable chamber works: Franz Schubert's beloved Trout Quintet, Charles Lefebvre's Wind Sextet, and Louise Farrenc's Nonet.

  • Franz Schubert Trout Quintet
  • Charles Lefebvre Wind Sextet
  • Louise Farrenc Nonet

Spanning the rich traditions of 19th-century chamber music, these works offer a fascinating exploration of musical dialogue, collaboration, and the unique personalities of different instrumental combinations.

Schubert's Trout Quintet is celebrated for its infectious energy, lyrical melodies, and playful character. Lefebvre's Wind Sextet invites us into a world of rich wind colours, intricate interplay, and expressive musical conversation. Farrenc's Nonet brings the strings and winds together in a vibrant and beautifully balanced ensemble, showcasing the possibilities of a larger chamber music collaboration.

There is a particularly interesting connection between the two French composers, Lefebvre and Farrenc, whose works offer a glimpse into the flourishing French chamber music tradition of the 19th century. Set alongside Schubert's distinctly individual voice, the three works create a musical journey across different styles, textures, and approaches to chamber music.

Together, these pieces embody everything the County Chamber Collective celebrates: musical curiosity, the joy of collaboration, artistic discovery, and the magic that happens when musicians come together to make something exceptional.
